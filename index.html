Scott Schaefer

Chair, Department of Economics, University of Utah

Division Chair, Quantitative Analysis of Markets and Organizations (QAMO), David Eccles School of Business

Special Advisor to the President

Dumke Family Presidential Endowed Chair in Honor of Katherine W. and E.R. Dumke, Jr.

Scott Schaefer is an economist who builds academic programs where business and economics meet. At Utah he chairs the Department of Economics and the Eccles School’s QAMO Division. There he designed a quantitative business-economics major, launched in 2017, and he is now leading the development of a new School of Economics. His research covers personnel economics and the economics of organization. He spent a decade on the faculty of Northwestern’s Kellogg School before joining Utah in 2005.
